Chat

One county-wide room, open to every signed-up member. Moderation runs automatically so the room stays usable.

Sign up free to join the chat

Chat is open to any free DelcoSpot account — just sign up or log in to start talking with your neighbors.

Room rules

  • No harassment, slurs, or threats
  • No addresses or phone numbers of private residents
  • No advertising or repeated link drops
  • Keep scanner speculation out of active incidents

How moderation works

Every message is screened automatically before it's posted. A blocked message never reaches the room. A second violation within 24 hours bans the account until a moderator lifts it.
